# Implementation Plan: Clean Inflow Header, Eliminate Repetition & Streamline Deeds

This plan addresses all items requested by the user: removing the live inflow stream header elements, explaining and removing the "Manage Deposits" button, replacing the active contract badge with the plan name, eliminating repetitive text on active cards, and removing the active tenure circle and icons under My Deeds.

---

## 1. Explanation & Removal of "Manage Deposits" & Live Inflow Desk Elements
**Target:** `src/components/RealInvestorDropCounter.tsx`, `src/components/AdminPanel.tsx`

### What "Manage Deposits" Does:
The **"Manage Deposits"** button was an administrative shortcut inside the real-time drop counter header. When clicked by an admin, it immediately switches the view to the **"Fast Approvals"** tab and pre-selects the **"Deposits"** filter. This allowed an administrator watching live investor incoming payments to quickly navigate to the deposit approval queue to review and approve manual bank transfer receipts and Paystack payment slips.

### Elements to Remove:
- **Live Liquidity Inflow Desk** badge.
- **Live Drop Stream** badge with the pulsing dot indicator.
- **Drop Chime ON / Chime Muted** sound toggle button (and associated audio chime trigger).
- **Manage Deposits** button and navigation callback.
- Streamline the remaining drop counter header to focus cleanly on the live investor drop statistics.

## 3. Eliminate Repetition on Active Contract Cards & Adjust Font Sizing
**Target:** `src/components/UserDashboard.tsx`
- **Current Issue:** The property name (e.g., "Sobha Sanctuary") is currently repeated 3 times across the top badge, the property title, and the allotted tier label, causing visual redundancy and font crowding.
- **Redesign & Hierarchy:**
  - **Top Left Badge:** Location pill: `🇦🇪 {prop.location}` (e.g. `🇦🇪 Dubai Marina`, `🇦🇪 MBR City`, `🇦🇪 Downtown Dubai`).

  - **Bottom Media Banner:**
    - Property Name: `{prop.propertyName}` (e.g., `Sobha Sanctuary Estates`) with refined font sizing (`text-sm sm:text-base font-extrabold`).

  - Reduced typography sizes and balanced line-heights to ensure all content fits comfortably inside the container across both mobile and desktop viewports without overflow.
